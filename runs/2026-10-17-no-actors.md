# Norway: actors and headquarters, 2026-10-17

## Scope

This was a separate actor review under `prompts/actors.md`. It did not inventory new capacity, alter project status or approve candidates.

The worklist contained 32 Norwegian projects that were not `out_of_scope`: 30 existing records and two candidates. Every project received at least one actor row. The result is 39 project and actor relationships covering 24 named actors. The two candidates remain P178 and P179.

The review added 21 source rows, S411 to S431. Existing source rows were preserved. Project and capacity files were not changed.

## Method

Project sources established the relationship to each site. Corporate reports, company pages, transaction announcements and official Norwegian entity records were then used to identify the operating group, role, business type, global headquarters and a short ownership description.

Local project companies were retained in notes when they represented the same operating group. They did not receive a second headquarters. This applies to WS Computing for Google, Aker Nscale for Nscale and the local Grenland Data Center project identity.

Named independent partners were retained where their role was material to the project. These include TikTok at Green Mountain OSL-Hamar, Frier Vest at BW Velora, Løvenskiold-Fossum at Nscale Bolvik, Wiig Gartneri at Green Horizon Orre, and the Magnora and Blix partners in Oslo and Halden.

The review used the same strict headquarters rule as Sweden and Finland. A registration country or local subsidiary address was not enough on its own. Two projects therefore have incomplete HQ classification:

- TikTok names both Los Angeles and Singapore as headquarters locations.
- Forsand DC AS is identified by the municipal planning notice, but no operating group or global headquarters has been published.

These actors remain visible. Their HQ fields are blank and the reason is recorded. A project can still match an HQ filter through another actor with a known headquarters.
