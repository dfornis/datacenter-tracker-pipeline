# Finland: actors and headquarters, 2026-10-17

## Scope

This was a separate actor review under `prompts/actors.md`. It did not inventory new capacity, alter project status or approve candidates.

The worklist contained 46 Finnish projects that were not `out_of_scope`: 42 existing records and four candidates. Every project received at least one actor row. The result is 51 project and actor relationships covering 29 named actors. The four candidates remain P159, P166, P167 and P168.

The review added 37 source rows, S374 to S410. Existing source rows were preserved. Project and capacity files were not changed.

## Method

Project sources established the relationship to each site. Corporate reports, company pages, transaction announcements and regulatory entity records were then used to identify the operating group, role, business type, global headquarters and a short ownership description.

Local project companies were kept in notes when they represented the same operating group. They did not receive a second headquarters. This applies to Tuike Finland, Hyperco Fin HoldCo 1, Data Prop Link, Data Tank Nordic and several other local entities.

Named project partners and customers were retained when they had a separate role. These are TikTok at DayOne Lahti, SDC Project Ventures at Pure DC Seinäjoki, Cerebras at Compute Nordic Mikkeli, Fortum at Nscale Harjavalta and Trevian at the GleSYS Oulu campus.

The review used a strict headquarters rule. A registration country or local subsidiary address was not enough on its own. Four actors therefore have no single HQ country in the register:

- TikTok names both Los Angeles and Singapore as headquarters locations.
- Compute Nordic lists several national offices but does not designate one global headquarters.
- AmpTank describes a Swiss-Finnish group but does not publish one global headquarters.
- Regant identifies the Finnish company and its work but publishes no physical headquarters.

These actors remain identified. Their HQ fields are blank and the reason is recorded. A project can still match an HQ filter through another actor with a known headquarters.
