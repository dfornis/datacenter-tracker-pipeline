# Status and decision interpretation

`docs/methodology.md` explains the method; `config/schema.json` contains the allowed values. Status is phase-specific. Preserve an operational parent project when evaluating an additional future phase.

| Decision basis | Interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| `explicit_decision` | An identified decision to execute this exact scope; record its terms and remaining conditions. |
| `execution_evidence` | Observed material execution of this scope, such as actual construction; distinguish this inference from an explicit FID. |
| `conditional_commitment` | A lease, partnership, financing or investment subject to conditions. Conditions are material evidence. |
| `no_public_evidence` | The documented search did not establish a decision. Use `investment_decision=unclear`, not an assertion that no decision exists. |
| `explicit_nondecision` | The source explicitly says the decision has not been made or the development has not been approved for execution. |
| `not_applicable` | A decision classification does not apply to this evidence item; explain why. |
| `legacy_unverified` | The old label has not yet been checked under this method. |

Project-level `yes` may describe an executing initial phase. It does not make a full-campus capacity claim committed. Capacity-level `yes` needs its own field-specific support. Permits, land acquisition, a press release, an anchor customer and a scheduled opening are different facts; none is an automatic FID proxy.

Use `Under construction` only for actual construction of the relevant scope. Preparatory land work may precede construction of the facility; describe the distinction. Keep a construction observation date if the start date is unknown. Never substitute publication/access year for construction start or construction start for commissioning.

Do not mark a project `Dormant` or `Cancelled` solely because a target date passed or a search found no update. Record an overdue date and unresolved current status. Distinguish a temporary delay, a withdrawn application, termination of one phase and cancellation of the entire campus.

Resolve conflicting statements by scope, authority and source version. Keep an unresolved conflict if the evidence cannot establish a single interpretation. A source's confidence is not a numerical probability of delivery. Preserve original quotations and record inferences explicitly.
